Frequently Asked Questions (1,500 Sq Ft Home & Attic Whole-House Fan & NFA Sizing)

How much attic venting (Net Free Area) do I need for 1,500 Sq Ft Home & Attic Whole-House Fan & NFA Sizing (3,750 CFM)?

Divide your total whole-house fan airflow (3,750 CFM) by 750 to get 5.0 square feet (720 square inches) of Net Free Vent Area (NFA). Because bug screens and louvers cut airflow in half, your gross cutout area across gable, dormer, O'Hagin, or ridge/soffit vents must be ~10.0 sq. ft.

How do I calculate passive attic ventilation under IRC Code R806.2 for a 1,500 sq ft attic?

Under the International Residential Code (IRC R806.2), standard attics require a 1:150 ratio (1,500 ÷ 150 = 10.0 sq. ft. / 1440 sq. in. NFA). If at least 40%–50% of the vents are high on the roof (ridge vents) and the remainder are low eave soffit vents, code allows a 1:300 ratio (720 sq. in. NFA).

How many 16x8 soffit vents and feet of ridge vent equal 720 sq in of NFA?

A standard 16"×8" aluminum soffit vent provides ~56 sq. in. of NFA, and a high-profile shingle-over ridge vent provides ~18 sq. in. of NFA per linear foot. Splitting 720 sq. in. 50/50 requires 7 soffit vents and 20 linear feet of ridge vent.
